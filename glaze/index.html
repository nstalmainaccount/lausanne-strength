Welcome

Welcome to the Lance Fein Glazing System in Beta

The Lance Fein Glazing System is a white-label comment curation system. Build a Glazing System below, then drop it into any website as a popup — just like sharing a Google Form link. Visitors rate comments, products, or services on a Likert scale of Lances, from + Five Lances down to − Five Lances, with Zero Lances being perfectly neutral.

Why "glazing"? Because slathering praise (or hate) on everything is easy. The Lance Fein Glazing System rewards restraint — and makes heavy glazing cost you.

Lance Score

Your Lance Score is made up of the following criteria:

  1. All your Persona's accumulated Lances (across any sites using a Glazing System).
  2. Behavioral data used to verify and establish user credibility.
  3. Proprietary formulas using comment data, glazing data, and time spent on site.

Your Lance Score is in many ways the on-line/digital equivalent of your real-world credit score. Just as companies such as Equifax and Experian use various criteria to determine how reliable you are as a consumer of credit, the Lance Fein Glazing System uses your Lance Score to determine how reliable you are as an author and consumer of digital commentary.

Glazing Basics

Before you score a comment, the author is invisible to you — you instead see a GLAZE FOR MORE ® box. GLAZE FOR MORE indicates that in order to get further information about this author, you must score the comment first.

GLAZE FOR MORE ® ▾

When you click on GLAZE FOR MORE, a drop-down with scoring options appears:

  • + Five Lances
  • + Four Lances
  • + Three Lances
  • + Two Lances
  • + One Lance
  • Zero Lances (Neutral)
  • − One Lance
  • − Two Lances
  • − Three Lances
  • − Four Lances
  • − Five Lances

Select the score you wish to give the comment and press the red Save button. The ribbon then reveals the author's name, their Lance Score, the specific score you gave, the total score for the comment, and the total amount of replies in the thread.

The credit mechanic — this is the whole point:

  • Score Zero Lances, One Lance, or Negative One Lance (a measured, honest take) → you earn One Lance Point toward your Lance Credit.
  • Score anything over + One Lance or under − One Lance (heavy glazing) → you lose One Lance Point.
  • Either way, the comment's author receives your Lances toward their Lance Score.

Every comment you score moves your Lance Credit. Rate generously often; rate extremely at your own expense.

Personas

Users may create up to Three Personas to uniquely identify their participation in a Glazing System.

A Persona is always displayed within a distinctive button along with their Lance Score badge. Each Persona may score comments and post messages independently of your other Personas — and each carries its own Lance Credit.
